# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We work with clarity, respect, patience, and technical honesty.

Expected behavior includes:

- Giving constructive, specific feedback
- Respecting project, client, and privacy boundaries
- Crediting upstream authors and collaborators
- Correcting mistakes without hiding evidence
- Making room for questions and different experience levels

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misattribution, disclosure of private information, or knowingly presenting unsupported claims as fact.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository or organization maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take proportionate action to protect the community.
